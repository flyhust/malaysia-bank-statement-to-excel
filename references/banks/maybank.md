# Maybank Malaysia (Maybank / Maybank Islamic — SME First, CCA, savings)

- Trilingual (Malay / Chinese / English).
- **The letterhead prints at the BOTTOM of each page.** Account number and statement date are at the end of the page text; the table body stops where `Malayan Banking Berhad` begins.
- One amount column. **Direction is the sign printed after the amount**: `1,800.00+` is money in, `1,800.00-` is money out.
- Balances may carry a `DR` suffix when overdrawn → negative.
- Payer name and customer note print on undated lines below the row, and that block **runs past the page break**, resuming after the next page's repeated column header.
